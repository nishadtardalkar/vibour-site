Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Vibour is an adults-only dating app. Users must be 18 or older. We prohibit sexual exploitation and abuse of children, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Standards

  • Accounts are limited to users 18+. Birth date is collected during onboarding.
  • We prohibit CSAM, grooming, and any sexual content involving minors.
  • We prohibit sharing sexual content of anyone without consent.
  • We use automated and human moderation of photos and text to keep the product SFW.
  • Users can block and report others from chat and profile surfaces.
  • We cooperate with lawful requests from authorities regarding child safety.

Reporting

In-app: use Report on a profile or chat. Email: support@vibour.com with subject “Child safety report”.

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement. In the U.S., you may also report to NCMEC CyberTipline.

Contact

Child safety contact: support@vibour.com · vibour.com
