Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Vibour (“we”, “us”) provides a quiz-first dating app. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using Vibour you agree to this policy.

Who this applies to

Vibour is intended for adults 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Information we collect

  • Account data — email and authentication identifiers when you sign in with email, Google, or Apple.
  • Profile data — display name, bio, birth date, gender, preference settings, city/location, and profile photos you upload.
  • Location — approximate or precise location (with permission) to power discovery distance filters.
  • Quiz and compatibility data — quiz answers, AI-generated personality summaries/embeddings used for matching, and paid compatibility narratives for matches.
  • Photos and verification — profile photos; optional face liveness / face-match verification to show a photo-verified badge (biometric processing for that purpose, with your consent).
  • Messages — chat is end-to-end encrypted (Signal Protocol). We store ciphertext envelopes needed to deliver messages; we cannot read message content.
  • Safety and moderation — reports, blocks, and automated moderation signals for photos and text.
  • Purchases — subscription and in-app purchase entitlements verified with Apple / Google; we do not store full payment card numbers.
  • Device and diagnostics — push notification tokens and limited technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

  • Provide discovery, matching, messaging, and account features
  • Verify photos, moderate SFW content, and handle reports/blocks
  • Process purchases and manage entitlements
  • Send transactional notifications (e.g. likes and matches)
  • Improve reliability, prevent fraud/abuse, and meet legal obligations

Sharing

We share profile information you choose to make discoverable with other users. We use cloud providers (including AWS) to host infrastructure. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account). Account deletion removes Cognito identity, profile PII, photos, and related personal data as described in our product documentation, with limited retention for trust & safety records (e.g. reports filed by others).

Your choices

  • Update profile and preference data in the app
  • Deny or revoke location, camera, or photo library permissions in system settings
  • Delete your account to request erasure of personal data we hold about you

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted transit and end-to-end encryption for chat content. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.
